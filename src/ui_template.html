PCHIP = shape-preserving cubic interpolation. Linear and PCHIP both use the same 72 measured hourly epochs, so agreement is a numerical sensitivity check—not independent proof of continuous illumination or communications. Alternate-hour withheld-sample errors quantify interpolation agreement only; they do not bound physical terrain or ephemeris error. A run reaching the first or last sample is boundary-truncated: the true physical window may extend outside the dataset. All dates are TDB, not New York local clock time.
Data and limitations: real 72-hour JPL Horizons DE441 Moon geometry, user-supplied Moonfix visibility/horizon pipeline, and NASA LOLA 80-m south-pole DEM. Sources: JPL Horizons · NASA LRO LOLA · NASA 2026 challenge. This is a research prototype, not NASA-sponsored operational software.
